Who we are

A Space to Connect

Latin Americanist Meetups (LAM) is a committee that organizes informal social gatherings at APSA and MPSA. Our goal is to create a relaxed and inclusive space for scholars doing research on Latin America to connect, meet new people, and continue conversations outside of panels and sessions. We meet every Friday of the conference.

How it works

At Every APSA & MPSA

We organize a happy hour at a venue near the conference hotel.

  • This is an inclusive, drop-in event; everyone is welcome to attend
  • No RSVP required
  • Each person covers their own drinks and food
  • Feel free to stop by, circulate among colleagues, and stay as long as you'd like

New · Virtual workshops

Junior Latin Americanist Workshops (JLAW)

The Junior Latin Americanist Workshops (JLAW) is an initiative that seeks to provide opportunities for junior scholars (ABD grad students, postdocs, and pre-tenure faculty) who study Latin American politics to virtually present and get feedback on their work and to network with other scholars with similar interests.

Applications open

We are now accepting applications for our Fall 2026 first workshops!

If you would like to present your work at a future JLAW session or volunteer to serve as a discussant, send your info to Vitória Sgorlon.

You can also write to her to sign up for our JLAW mailing list and receive updates on our work, or with any comments or questions.

Email Vitória Sgorlon

vitoria.sgorlon@stonybrook.edu

The format

  • Authors present their paper for 10–15 minutes
  • Discussants offer comments for 5–10 minutes
  • Finally, a Q&A

We strongly encourage participants to read the papers in advance since the presentation is short! Our workshops will happen once a month.

Call for papers and discussants

We seek submissions of article-length manuscripts from all subfields. Submissions may be solo or collaborative.

We are also looking for discussants (junior or senior) to provide feedback.

Everyone is welcome

Even though our goal is to showcase and support the work of junior scholars, we welcome everyone in the discipline to attend our events! It can be especially interesting for those on hiring committees seeking to learn about those in the job market.
